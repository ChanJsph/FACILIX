Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a maintenance request? ▼

Navigate to the "Requests" section and click "New Request". Fill in the required details about your maintenance need, select the service category, and submit. Our team will review and contact you within 24 hours.

Can I track the status of my requests? ▼

Yes! Visit the "Requests" page to see all your submitted requests with real-time status updates. You can filter by status, date, and department.

What services does FACILIX offer? ▼

FACILIX offers comprehensive facility management services including HVAC maintenance, plumbing, electrical services, cleaning, security systems, and pest control. Visit the "Services" page to see our full offering.

How can I upgrade my plan? ▼

Go to the "Subscription" tab to view available plans. Click "Change Plan" to upgrade or downgrade your subscription. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is my data secure? ▼

Yes! FACILIX uses enterprise-grade encryption and security measures to protect your data. We comply with industry standards and regularly conduct security audits.

How do I get technical support? ▼

Our support team is available 24/7. Click "Open Support Ticket" above, email support@facilix.com, or call 1-800-FACILIX for immediate assistance.
